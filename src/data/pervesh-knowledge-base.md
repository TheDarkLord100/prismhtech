# Pervesh Rasayan Pvt. Ltd. — Knowledge Base

## Company Overview

**Pervesh Rasayan Pvt. Ltd.** is an industrial chemical supplier and distributor based in Faridabad, Haryana, India.

The company was founded in **1984** by **Shri M.L. Kakar** and has over **41 years of experience** supplying industrial, electroplating, specialty, laboratory, and water treatment chemicals to businesses across India.

Pervesh Rasayan focuses on delivering reliable chemical solutions while building long-term relationships based on quality, trust, and consistency.

# Product Categories Explained

## 1. Electroplating Chemicals

Electroplating chemicals are used to deposit a protective or decorative metal coating on another metal surface.

### Applications

- Corrosion resistance
- Improved appearance
- Conductivity enhancement
- Surface finishing
- Industrial durability

### Industries

- Automobile
- Manufacturing
- Hardware
- Metal finishing units

---

## 2. Phosphating Chemicals

Phosphating chemicals create a phosphate coating on metal surfaces.

### Benefits

- Prevent rust.
- Improve paint adhesion.
- Prepare metal before coating.
- Increase corrosion resistance.

### Applications

- Automotive components
- Steel fabrication
- Industrial machinery
- Surface preparation

- Chemical manufacturing
- Industrial processing
